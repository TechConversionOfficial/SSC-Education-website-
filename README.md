## The problem

Prospective students typically do not search for "computer education" as one generic product. One student wants basic digital literacy, another needs Tally and GST for accounting, another is considering Python or web development, and a job seeker may be comparing Advanced Excel, data entry and short diploma programmes.

A training website needs to answer **which course suits me, what will I learn, how long does it take, and how do I enquire?** If those answers are scattered across a long page or bundled with unrelated laptop-sales information, students may find it difficult to make an informed choice. Parents and first-time learners also need reassurance about classes, faculty, fees, training format and certification, without unsupported placement guarantees.

The saved designs show a deliberate response to these information needs. No independently dated audit of an earlier production education website was available, so this describes the project objective rather than a measured flaw in a previous version.

## How the website design addresses the student journey

The following items describe **features visible in SSC-specific project layouts**. They are not a claim that every design element has been deployed or that unseen integrations were tested.

1. **Lead with practical computer education.** The SSC Haveri homepage places the training proposition, courses and enrolment actions prominently above broader group services.
2. **Show popular programmes early.** DCA, PGDCA, office skills, Advanced Excel, Python, Tally and other entry points appear as recognisable course cards with concise labels and proposed durations.
3. **Organise the catalogue around intent.** Saved course layouts include goal-based choices such as starting with computer basics, preparing for an office job, learning accounting or beginning programming.
4. **Make course comparisons easier.** Search, level, category and duration controls appear in saved project screens, giving students several ways to narrow a broad course catalogue.
5. **Create a separate course-bundle pathway.** Some designs group complementary skills, such as office productivity or computer applications plus programming, to illustrate possible learning paths. Offers and discounts visible in drafts require verification.
6. **Explain the learning process.** Practical classroom training, assessments, certification guidance and career support are shown as distinct parts of the student journey, not merely decorative claims.
7. **Keep admissions actions visible.** Buttons such as *Explore Courses*, *Enquire Now* and *Book Free Counselling* appear near key decision points. Their presence in the artwork does not confirm that every live form or communication integration is operating.
8. **Present the wider SSC ecosystem without losing focus.** Group service links may assist visitors interested in laptops or repairs, but the education project gives priority to courses, suitability and admissions.
9. **Plan for mobile reading.** The long-page compositions use compact cards, clear calls to action and modular sections; full device testing and accessibility compliance have not been independently documented.

## Standards the website should be reviewed against

The following are **review criteria**, not a statement of completed certification, audits or successful independent testing.

- **[Core Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals):** check large hero images, course-grid load time, layout shifts and mobile interaction responsiveness.
- **[WCAG 2.2 Level AA](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/):** evaluate keyboard-friendly course filters, readable labels, focus indication, accessible forms and text contrast.
- **[Google Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials):** review unique course descriptions, clear headings, discoverable course pages and meaningful internal links.
- **[Schema.org](https://schema.org/):** evaluate accurate EducationalOrganization, Course, BreadcrumbList and other relevant structured data where supported by factual content.
- **Credential accuracy:** confirm the exact issuing organisation, course recognition or affiliation, validity, location and applicable certification scope before displaying government/KEONICS badges or asserting universal recognition.
- **Admissions accuracy:** verify current fees, duration, eligibility, mode, admissions dates, terms and certificate award requirements.
- **Career-claims accuracy:** distinguish placement assistance from placement guarantees; validate student counts, testimonials and employment outcomes before publication.
- **Privacy:** review applicable treatment of prospective students' names, phone numbers, education history and contact-form submissions.
