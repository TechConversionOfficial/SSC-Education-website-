## About SSC Education

SSC Haveri's education identity appears across project materials as **SSC Haveri** and **Shree Shivlingeshwar Computers**, with a focus on practical training and career-oriented computer skills. It sits within a broader SSC business presence that also covers laptops, accessories, servicing and CCTV solutions. The **SSC Education** portfolio item specifically concerns the *training and admissions journey* and should not be confused with separate SSC ecommerce or group-business projects.

Project screens show subjects such as Diploma in Computer Application (DCA), PGDCA, computer fundamentals, MS Office, Advanced Excel, Tally Prime with GST, Python, C programming, web development and data-entry or typing skills. Course durations, prices, offers and experience levels visible in any artwork should be treated as project content until confirmed against a current prospectus.

Tech Conversion describes the project as a government-recognised training website. Project materials contain KEONICS and other recognition references; however, the exact current accreditation, recognition scope and validity for each course or branch should be verified directly with the institute and the relevant issuing body. An independently published KEONICS centre list includes an SSC Solutions training location in **Hangal, Haveri district**; that does not establish the accreditation status of every programme shown in project visuals.

## The problem

Prospective students typically do not search for "computer education" as one generic product. One student wants basic digital literacy, another needs Tally and GST for accounting, another is considering Python or web development, and a job seeker may be comparing Advanced Excel, data entry and short diploma programmes.

A training website needs to answer **which course suits me, what will I learn, how long does it take, and how do I enquire?** If those answers are scattered across a long page or bundled with unrelated laptop-sales information, students may find it difficult to make an informed choice. Parents and first-time learners also need reassurance about classes, faculty, fees, training format and certification without unsupported placement guarantees.

The project materials show a deliberate response to these information needs. They do not, by themselves, establish measured shortcomings of any earlier live website.

## How the website design addresses the student journey

The following points describe **features visible in SSC-specific project layouts**. They are not a claim that every design element has been deployed or that unseen integrations were independently tested.

1. **Lead with practical computer education.** The SSC Haveri education experience places the training proposition, courses and enrolment actions prominently above broader group services.
2. **Show popular programmes early.** DCA, PGDCA, office skills, Advanced Excel, Python, Tally and other entry points appear as recognisable course cards.
3. **Organise the catalogue around intent.** Course layouts use goal-based choices such as starting with computer basics, preparing for an office job, learning accounting or beginning programming.
4. **Make course comparisons easier.** Search, level, category and duration controls help students narrow a broad course catalogue.
5. **Create learning-path options.** Some designs group complementary skills, such as office productivity or computer applications plus programming, to illustrate possible learning paths. Any offers or discounts require current verification.
6. **Explain the learning process.** Practical classroom training, assessments, certification guidance and career support are presented as distinct parts of the student journey.
7. **Keep admissions actions visible.** Calls to action such as *Explore Courses*, *Enquire Now* and *Book Free Counselling* support the transition from course research to an enquiry.
8. **Present the wider SSC ecosystem without losing focus.** Group service links may assist visitors interested in laptops or repairs, while the education project keeps priority on courses, suitability and admissions.
9. **Support mobile reading.** Modular sections, compact cards and visible calls to action are appropriate for smaller screens, although no independent accessibility or device audit is claimed here.

## Standards the website should be reviewed against

The following are **review criteria**, not a statement of completed certification, audits or successful independent testing.

- **[Core Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals):** check large hero images, course-grid load time, layout shifts and mobile interaction responsiveness.
- **[WCAG 2.2 Level AA](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/):** evaluate keyboard-friendly course filters, readable labels, focus indication, accessible forms and text contrast.
- **[Google Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials):** review unique course descriptions, clear headings, discoverable course pages and meaningful internal links.
- **[Schema.org](https://schema.org/):** evaluate accurate EducationalOrganization, Course, BreadcrumbList and other relevant structured data where supported by factual content.
- **Credential accuracy:** confirm the exact issuing organisation, course recognition or affiliation, validity, location and applicable certification scope before displaying government/KEONICS badges or asserting universal recognition.
- **Admissions accuracy:** verify current fees, duration, eligibility, mode, admissions dates, terms and certificate-award requirements.
- **Career-claims accuracy:** distinguish placement assistance from placement guarantees; validate student counts, testimonials and employment outcomes before publication.
- **Privacy:** review applicable treatment of prospective students' names, phone numbers, education history and contact-form submissions.
